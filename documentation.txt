Problem statement: Students and staff at the university campus face long wait times and unorganized queues at the canteen, leading to wasted time and a frustrating dining experience during peak hours.

Target users:
- University Students
- Campus Staff and Faculty

Features implemented:
- Digital Menu & Ordering: Browse canteen food items and place orders seamlessly.
- Smart Wallet System: Pre-load balance for instant one-click payments.
- Live Order Tracking: Real-time status updates (Accepted, Preparing, Ready) and a dynamic AI predicted ready time.
- Queue Management: Live queue placement tracker showing the user's exact position.
- Digital Pick-up Tickets: Secure QR code and token-based digital tickets for easy counter pickup.
- Cancellations: Option to cancel an order within a 2-minute window.
- Notifications: Push alerts when the order is ready for pickup.
